# Kernel Panic

**Play it: https://kernel-panic.higgsfield.app** (browser, no account, no install)

You inherited your father's computer repair shop, and the machine in the back room he never let you touch. Fix customers' devices for ten days, get stronger each night, and reach the machine.

## How to play

1. **Log in.** Pick a save slot on the KP/OS login screen. The first run starts with a guided dive that teaches the controls in about two minutes.
2. **Open the shop.** Click OPEN THE SHOP in the SHOPFRONT window, then read the day's log with NEXT.
3. **Take a job.** Open INBOX on the desktop, click a ticket to read the customer's request and the diagnostic (what the intrusion likes to do), then click DIVE INTO DEVICE. Three tickets a day.
4. **Win the dive.** Two boards, yours and the machine's. Click a glowing junction on your board to rotate it (1 RAM). Line the pipes up so your signal reaches the goal on the right before the machine reaches its own. Chains of three or more lit junctions bank bonus RAM for next turn.
   - **SCAN** reveals the machine's armed traps near your ground.
   - **ATTACK** reaches across to its board (REDIRECT twists one of its junctions).
   - **DEFEND** guards your own board (PURGE clears a trap).
   - **END TURN** (E) hands the turn to the machine. **UNDO** (Z) takes back your last move.
5. **Watch your strain.** Going over par or springing a trap costs Neural Strain, and strain is shared across the whole day. Zero ends the run.
6. **Get paid.** REPAIR.LOG shows the receipt and lets you claim one augment from the day's cache.
7. **Close the night.** After the third ticket, NIGHT.SYS offers one free upgrade (more RAM or a program tier) and a night shop. DARKNET.LNK sells blind patch pulls; SOLDER.BAY turns patches into pieces you can place on the board.
8. **Repeat for ten days.** Read DAD.LOG as files recover, check MANUAL.TXT for the full rules, and LEDGER.LOG for your numbers. On day ten, the back room opens.

## Tips

- Configure your kit (LOADOUT.CFG) against the diagnostic before you dive, not after.
- Scan early against siphon and halt traps; purge before you route through them.
- Losing a dive ends the run, but the next attempt keeps what you learned.
