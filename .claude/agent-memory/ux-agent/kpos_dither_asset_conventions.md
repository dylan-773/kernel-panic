- **304x304 is the house size for a square 1-bit dither cell.** It has now been used three separate times for unrelated art (the SOLDER.BAY bench illustration banked in `ui-demos/kpos-shell/art/solder-bench-*.png`, LOADOUT.CFG's FIG.01/BENCH FEED cells, and the ui-integration-2026-07-29 cycle's CUSTOMER.REC portrait + device-macro pair). Default new square dither-cell art orders to this size unless a specific window's layout math genuinely demands something else, and say so explicitly if it does. Non-square cells (the REPAIR.LOG figure card, 162x234) are the destination cell's own exact inner CSS size, not a variant of the house square — always confirm the actual rendered cell size rather than assuming 304 applies to a non-square frame.
- **Before ordering a re-treatment of existing art for the ink-tint multiply pass, look at the actual image first.** For ui-integration-2026-07-29's DesktopIdle/story-still question, reading `still-locked.png` directly (not just reasoning about the treatment in the abstract) showed strong luminance-based separation (a warm lit focal point against desaturated blue-grey), which grayscale+multiply reads cleanly — so no re-treatment order was filed, with the specific visual reasoning stated in the proposal's notes rather than a blanket "should be fine" guess. **Why:** ordering blind re-treatments for existing shipped art burns art-lead budget and credits on a problem that may not exist; a quick visual check costs nothing. **How to apply:** any time a brief asks whether existing pixel-treatment art "survives" a new tinting/dithering pipeline, actually Read one representative image before deciding whether to file a re-treatment order, and name what was checked in the proposal notes.
- **When an art budget cannot cover a full customer/content roster in one cycle, prioritize by what is already live and broken, not by roster order.** ui-integration-2026-07-29 had 12 customers needing CUSTOMER.REC card art but a 60-credit cap; the highest-value orders were the 3 customers two already-shipped studies (INBOX, REPAIR.LOG) were placeholder-borrowing Dex Marlowe's art for, since that closes an active wrong-face bug on two live windows at once, ahead of broadening roster coverage for customers nobody has seen wrong art for yet. State the prioritization reasoning and the deferred remainder explicitly in the proposal's notes so a future cycle knows the roster gap is known, not forgotten.
