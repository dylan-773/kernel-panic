On 2026-07-25 the user adjudicated open canon questions and the Loremaster is
recording three rulings in `lore/ledger.md` / `lore/bible.md` that override
older shipped copy and older ledger Open Questions:

1. **The mother is excised from canon entirely.** There is no "she", ever,
   referring to a parent. Dad raised the kids alone. The family's shape is
   never explained on screen. This retires ledger Open Question 1 ("who she
   is in sector 2") as moot rather than answered.
2. **No dust in the shop.** The back room and the shop generally were buried
   in Dad's clutter, not decades of dust. Dad was a messy genius; Rhea has
   been cleaning up since he died. The gap between Dad's death and run 1 is
   short: days to weeks, not years. This retires ledger Open Question 7 (the
   dust-vs-nightly-diving timeline tension) by removing dust as a motif
   entirely rather than trying to reconcile it.
3. **No family surname in canon.** "Overby" is gone. Paperwork (Meridian
   bills, etc.) is cold and account-styled: account numbers, not names. This
   retires ledger Open Question 8.

**Why this matters going forward:** any new journal/scene/dayline copy I
write must never reintroduce "she" for a parent, must never use "dust" as a
shorthand for the back room being sealed/forgotten (use clutter, mess,
Dad's piles instead), and must never invent or restate a surname for the
family. Paperwork props (bills, notices, forms) should read as account
numbers or codes, never names.
