# Ground truth

> [!danger] Total spoilers
> This is what actually happened, in full. The player assembles it out of the shop, in whatever order they repair it. Nothing here may reach the player faster than [[reveal-schedule]] allows.

## What happened to Dad

Six steps. These are cited elsewhere as the numbered ground truth.

1. **He raised his son alone.** The family's shape is never explained and never will be. There is no mother in canon. See [[ruling-01-the-mother]]. He began spending his nights with the machine, building something for his son.
2. **He built and trained an AI** by diving his own back-room tower nightly after close, for years. **9,000+ logged hours.** No client, no ticket, no pay.
3. **The hours scarred him.** NF-3 neurofilament degradation, stage three. He hid it: weekly cash suppressants for six years from the pharmacy on 9th, and eleven unpaid Meridian Neurocare bills filed under W.
4. **Ordered to CEASE ALL DIVE ACTIVITY IMMEDIATELY, he kept diving.** Once the diagnosis landed, finishing [[patch]] stopped being a project and became the inheritance. He would not leave his son with nothing, and he had faith his son would be smart enough to reach what he built. He read the order at the clinic, decided it changed nothing, came home and made dinner. The last suppressant receipt is dated **four days before he died**. The strain killed him: "not an accident and it was not a job."
5. **He recorded fragments of himself into the machine** as cargo, nine sectors, and left a final message. He sealed the back room himself with a standing order: open only for a fair, assist-free win by his son. *"Not until he can beat you square. Promise me."*
6. **The will leaves the whole shop to the son**, counter and bench both, with one line about the room: "The back room stays locked until it does not."

## What Patch is

The AI Dad built **for his son**. Not a virus, not a weapon, not a tool.

It shares the same substrate as every intrusion in the city, a living AI inside a machine ([[ruling-05-nature-of-intrusions]]), but it was **built and raised, not caught**. "Not a virus" is hard law regardless.

Gatekeeper, sparring partner, curriculum. It paces difficulty to the player and never throws a game. It was the opponent in every back-room dive: *"that was me across the grid. I never once let you win."*

Custodian of sectors 1 to 7, and of **sector 8**, the day Dad died, withheld as its own grief rather than training cargo. The finale message is **sector 9**. See [[ruling-03-sectors-8-and-9]] and [[ruling-04-finale-mechanism]].

Named under Dad's house rule that everything in the shop gets a name: *the thing that holds a broken thing together while it mends.*

## What the player believes

- **At the start:** that the back room holds a quarantined virus. Nobody told him so; he inferred it, and the inference is a good one, because viruses here are living AI and that is the ordinary explanation for an extraordinary locked machine. See [[ruling-17-the-players-own-assumption]].
- **Knows from childhood:** Dad spent nights in that room for years, talking through the wall. He thought it was the radio. Dad came out smiling.
- **Learns by fixing the place:** every repair turns up something of his father's, and the evidence accumulates in whatever order the player works. See [[repairs-and-unlocks]].
- **Never reaches ground truth before a win.** No artifact gives Patch's name, the diagnosis in Dad's own words, or the seal condition stated outright. The renunciation of his own theory is his to arrive at, and it is authored as a [[sunday]] scene.

## The seal

Opens only on **"A FAIR WIN, NO ASSISTS"**. It is a lock in software and nothing else: there is no padlock, no chain, and never was. The room is open; the machine is not. See [[ruling-15-the-seal-is-software]].
