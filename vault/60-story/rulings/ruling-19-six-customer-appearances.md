# Ruling 19 - SIX CUSTOMER APPEARANCES

*Decided 2026-08-19, gate ruling, closing the gap [[customers]] already flagged ("An unlisted customer has no likeness yet. Invent nothing without a ruling.") The walkable room needs all twelve on screen, not just the five with portrait assets.*

Each line follows the roster's existing form: a role or type, one or two concrete physical or costume details, a closing note of mood or habit. Derived from each customer's device, their One Wow line, and [[world-overview]]'s districts, clinics, pawn shops, night pharmacies and lifter exosuits.

| Customer | Appearance |
|---|---|
| [[june-aksoy]] | Night-shift nurse, scrubs under an open coat, badge reel swinging, running on the shift's last coffee. |
| [[ines-calloway]] | Freight loader, exosuit rig still half strapped on, calloused hands, braced like the suit might move again on its own. |
| [[emeric-snow]] | Elderly chess club regular, cardigan buttoned off by one, glasses on a chain, unsettled by something he cannot name. |
| [[vera-stanek]] | Night pharmacist, white coat over street clothes, keys on a coiled wrist strap, counts under her breath out of habit. |
| [[casimir-bell]] | Pawnbroker, rings on every finger, jeweler's loupe on a cord, watches hands more than faces out of habit. |
| [[noor-behzadi]] | Touring musician, kit bag never fully unpacked, undercut and smudged eyeliner, talks about the theft like she is reviewing a show. |

Notes on the derivation, for whoever orders the art:

- **June Aksoy** guards a clinic gateway that "walls off a hospital ward at a time, at shift change." A nurse who works that shift is the honest read, not a fixer hired to babysit hardware.
- **Ines Calloway** brings in a lifter exosuit that "cuts servos at the worst second, like a test." She works in it, which is why she is still half strapped into the rig at the counter.
- **Emeric Snow**'s chess cabinet "stopped playing chess and started playing him." A club regular, not a prodigy, because the complaint is about being outplayed by something that used to lose politely.
- **Vera Stanek**'s dosage safe "rations the dispensary's power like pills," which rhymes with [[dad]]'s own suppressant prescriptions. She is staff at the counter it rations from, not a patient.
- **Casimir Bell**'s pawn vault "grew a lock nobody bought." A pawnbroker reads hands and collateral for a living before he reads a face, which is the detail that carries the line.
- **Noor Behzadi**'s synth "performs her sets while she sleeps. Colder." A touring musician whose instrument is doing her job without her is the whole complaint in a costume.

## Forbids

Any redesign of these six once art exists, without a new ruling. Any likeness for the six that contradicts the device, tier band or dominant mode already fixed in [[customers]].

## Permits

Portrait and room-figure art to proceed from these lines exactly as the prior five customers' appearance rulings were used for `cust-card-*-portrait.png` and room-figure assets.

## Integration

Each line is copied into its customer note under an Appearance heading, replacing "Not yet ruled. Invent nothing without a ruling." [[customers]]' Appearance canon section is updated: all twelve are now ruled.
