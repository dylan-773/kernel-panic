# Casimir Bell

**Ledgerstone pawn vault** · tiers 4, 5 · dominant [[lock]] · `casimir-bell`

> The vault grew a lock nobody bought.

## Appearance

*Canon, ruled 2026-08-19, [[ruling-19-six-customer-appearances]].* Pawnbroker, rings on every finger, jeweler's loupe on a cord, watches hands more than faces out of habit.

## The dive

Top-tier clamps. At `DEFEND_WIDTH` 3 it freezes three junctions per cast for two rounds each, which can stall a route entirely if the player has no [[cascades-and-surge|cascade]] ready.

## The line

"A lock nobody bought" is the whole game in five words, and it is truer now than when it was written: the thing in the back room is a lock with no lock, software holding a door that opens fine.

Of the eleven customer hooks that echo the main story, this is the least disguised, which is why it sits at the top of the tier band: by the time a shop is being sent work like this, the player has been thinking about the back room for a while.
