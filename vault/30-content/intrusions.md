# Intrusions

**Viruses in this world are AI: dynamic and alive**, infecting machines the way biological viruses infect a body.

Clearing one is not deleting static code. It fights back. That is why every repair is a dive, and why every shop ticket is an intrusion job.

## The epidemic

Mundane and unexplained. **No causal link to [[patch]], [[dad]], or the shop.** See [[ruling-05-nature-of-intrusions]].

That "no causal link" clause is doing real work. It keeps the game's premise from collapsing into "your father caused the plague", which would make the story about guilt rather than grief.

## Behaviour

Opponents inside devices are **scripted intrusions with one dominant mode each**. The Analyze diagnostic's tell is **always honest**.

That honesty is a design law, not a courtesy. It makes the pre-dive read a real decision: a `redirect` job and an `armHalt` job want different kits. See [[traps-and-telegraphs]].

## The diagnostic tag

Labelled **INTRUSION** in [[dive-exe]], always, on every dive including the finale. It is the world's own diagnostic class for any AI signal occupying a device that is not yours. Never a name, never a pronoun. See [[ruling-11-opponent-identity-tag]].

## Patch shares the substrate

[[patch]] is a living AI in a machine, the same kind of thing. It was **built and raised, not caught**. "Not a virus" is hard law regardless.

This is the fact [[rhea]] does not have, and the reason her guess was reasonable. See [[ruling-02-cover-story]].
