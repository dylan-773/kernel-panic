# Ruling 15 - THE SEAL IS SOFTWARE

*Decided 2026-08-16, user call, during the day-as-run redesign.*

**There is no padlock. There is no curtain. There is no physical barrier of any kind on the back room.** The door is a door and the player can walk through it whenever they like.

What bars the player is the machine itself: a lock in software, on the drive, held by what is inside it. The player is not locked out of a room. They are locked out of a computer.

**Forbids:** any padlock, chain, key, bolt or curtain in copy, art or scene direction; any line describing the seal as a physical object being opened, cut or broken.

**Permits:** the room being unremarkable and open, which is stranger than a lock and cheaper to justify. It also makes the refusal personal: something decided that you may not see this, and it decides again every time you try.

**Carried from the deleted ruling 7:** **no dust.** The shop is buried in [[dad]]'s clutter because he was a messy genius, and the gap between his death and the first day is **short, days to weeks**. Forbids any "decade of dust" or long post-death framing. What changed is who clears the clutter: the player does, and clearing it is now the core loop. See [[repairs-and-unlocks]].

**Integration:** the shipped opener line "The padlock is the only thing in this shop Dad ever put away properly" loses its subject and needs a new one. The tower itself is the obvious referent.
