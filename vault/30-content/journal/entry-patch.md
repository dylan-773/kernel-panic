# PATCH

`PATCH.SYS` · SYS · **`requiresOpened`** · kind `note`

*Provenance: full volume unlocked, recovered whole, the morning after.*

> [!danger] Finale-gated
> `unlockAtRun: 0` **plus** `requiresOpened: true`. It is invisible until `machineOpened` is true on `MetaState`, then available immediately regardless of `runCount`. A win on any run delivers it.

## What it carries

The name, the reason for it, and the whole truth.

> the thing that holds a broken thing together while it mends

## The only SYS doctype

Every other file in the archive is something recovered in fragments: scans, partial recoveries, telemetry. This one is **recovered whole**.

The volume is not damaged any more. That is the reward, stated as a file property rather than as a line of dialogue.

## "The morning after"

Not the moment of the win. The finale scene handles that. This is the next day, at the bench, reading it properly.

## The lowercase coincidence pays off

"patch" has been an ordinary noun for the entire game: Night Patch, patch pieces, patching a board. The coincidence is allowed to sit unremarked before the finale, and is never pointed at afterwards either. See [[brands-and-glossary]].
