# Vera Stanek

**Apothek dosage safe** · tiers 4, 5 · dominant [[arm-siphon|armSiphon]] · `vera-stanek`

> It rations the dispensary's power like pills.

## Appearance

Not yet ruled. **Invent nothing without a ruling.**

## The dive

Top-tier siphon. At ATTACK tier 3 it moves 4 [[ram]] per trap out of your turn and into its own, which can decide a dive by itself.

## The rhyme

A machine that rations supply. [[dad]] bought weekly strain suppressants in cash for six years from the pharmacy on 9th, on a climbing dosage, and the last receipt is dated four days before he died.

A dosage safe that "rations like pills" is the roster's coldest line, and it sits at tiers 4 and 5, so it arrives late in the shop's life rather than late in a run. Whether the player has read [[entry-receipts|RECEIPTS]] by then is no longer guaranteed, and the line does not need it to land.
