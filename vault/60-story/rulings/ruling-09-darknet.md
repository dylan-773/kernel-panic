# Ruling 9 - DARKNET

*Added 2026-07-28, Loremaster gate on the deep-balance cycle. Ruled a gap the brief exposed, not a direct user call.*

A gray-market trade in [[patch-pieces|patch pieces]] exists in the city: salvage off scrapped and dead machines, reachable by signal only **after the shop closes**.

**The dealer is permanently anonymous.** No name, no ID on file. No causal link to [[patch]], [[dad]], or the back room, following the same pattern as [[ruling-05-nature-of-intrusions]].

**Forbids:** any named character attaching to this market without a later explicit ruling.

**Hook, not a commitment:** the dealer's cash-only anonymity may mirror Dad's own secrecy in a future arc.

> [!info] The constraint made the design
> Because canon forbids a person, [[darknet-lnk]] carries its entire characterization through interface: a dial handshake over three relay hops, notched chrome, a blind price. See [[the-darknet]].
