# June Aksoy

**Halcyon clinic gateway** · tiers 3, 4 · dominant [[ward]] · `june-aksoy`

> It walls off a hospital ward at a time, at shift change.

## Appearance

Not yet ruled. **Invent nothing without a ruling.**

## The dive

High-tier warding. At `DEFEND_WIDTH` 2 or 3 and radius 2 or 3, whole approach corridors refuse your traps and shrug off your redirects.

## The pun is load-bearing

The mechanic is called WARD. The device guards hospital wards. The intrusion walls off one at a time.

It is the roster's sharpest piece of naming, and it is also the only customer whose complaint has stakes beyond property: "at shift change" means people. The game does not dwell on it.
