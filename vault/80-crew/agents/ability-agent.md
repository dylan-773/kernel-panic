# Ability Agent

The augment and mode catalog. Sonnet, `maxTurns` 20, `memory: project`.

## Owns

`pipeline/proposals/ability-agent.json`.

## The brief

Augments and modes proposed as **synergy and counter pairs**, always driven by the latest [[validation]] report.

Pairs rather than singles, because a new augment that nothing answers is a balance problem shipped as content. [[lock]] and the SURGE shatter are the model: a denial and its counter, designed together.

## Iron rule 4 applies

Ability changes enter **only through the balance loop, with before and after sim numbers**. A proposal with no numbers is not a proposal.
