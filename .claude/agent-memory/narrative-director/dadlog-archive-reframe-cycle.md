Cycle `ux-2026-07-29-dadlog` reframed DAD.LOG from "the player's own journal"
into DAD.VOL, Dad's read-only recovered volume, per an explicit user
directive quoted in `pipeline/BRIEF.md`. My deliverable was narrower than my
usual charter: fulfill `pipeline/copy/orders/copy-dadlog-files.json` only
(re-author all 10 journal entries as artifacts: filename, doctype,
provenance, title, body, optional benchNote, plus viewer chrome lines). No
`pipeline/proposals/narrative-director.json` this cycle, no art order (art
budget was zero, banked dithers only), and `kernel-panic-site/`/`lore/` stay
untouched (this was a standalone study in `ui-demos/kpos-shell/`, main-game
integration deferred).

This reframe is now canon: `lore/ledger.md` Resolved ruling 12 (DAD.LOG
REFRAME) records the presentation-fiction change and, critically, states the
generalizable rule both gate rounds cited: **a document body may only say
what its own diegetic source could plausibly know or print; comparison,
inference, and reaction are the player's own voice and belong in the bench
annotation, never the artifact's printed text.** Ruling 13 (DAD.VOL
PROVENANCE) separately settled that the tower's own drive can coherently hold
both Dad's historical files and the player's own live session telemetry,
since the tower is unticketed and off the shop's separate ticketing system.

Two round-2 fixes worth remembering as durable craft lessons, not just
one-off bugs: see [[artifact-voice-vs-benchnote-split]] and
[[diegetic-cadence-claims-need-checking]].

My SEGMENT-not-SECTOR vocabulary call (coining a distinct noun for DAD.LOG's
generic file-recovery unit so it never collides with the story's numbered
Sector 1-9 cargo, which the bible's own system-voice example already
reserves) was APPROVED outright at the loremaster gate with no ruling needed;
it followed directly from an existing bible line. Good instinct to keep:
when two systems could plausibly share a word, check whether the shared word
already carries story weight elsewhere before reusing it, and coin a new one
if it does.
