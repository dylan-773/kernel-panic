# CHEAP SHOT

**Boost** · `cheapShot`

> Your first ATTACK each dive costs 0 RAM.

Reads `econ.attacksCast`. Worth exactly 1 RAM per dive, which sounds small and is not: an opening turn with a free [[redirect]] converts directly into rotations, and opening tempo is what the [[the-board]] generator is calibrated around.
