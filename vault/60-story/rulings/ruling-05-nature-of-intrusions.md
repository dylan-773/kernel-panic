# Ruling 5 - NATURE OF INTRUSIONS

*Decided 2026-07-25, user call.*

Viruses in this world are **AI, dynamic and alive**, infecting machines the way biological viruses infect a body. Clearing one fights back, which is why every job is a dive.

The epidemic is **mundane and unexplained**, with **no causal link** to [[patch]], [[dad]], or the shop.

[[patch]] shares the substrate, a living AI in a machine, but was **built and raised, not caught**. "Not a virus" stays hard law regardless.

**What this buys:** [[rhea]]'s virus theory was always a reasonable in-world inference rather than naivety, which supports playing her erosion as honest. See [[ruling-02-cover-story]].
