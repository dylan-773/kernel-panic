## horizon 2

The machine now also scores by **how much of the player's live grid goes dark**. Cuts stop being about route cost alone and start being about tempo damage.

This is exactly what the day line points at: "The intrusions are pacing themselves now." The fiction and the dial are the same fact, which is the arc's method throughout. See [[the-machine]].
