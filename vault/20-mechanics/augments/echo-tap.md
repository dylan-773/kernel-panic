# ECHO TAP

**Boost** · `echoTap`

> Whenever one of your traps fires, gain 2 RAM on your next turn.

Fires for either trap kind, so it works with [[arm-halt]] and [[arm-siphon]] both. Arrives through `drainNext` as a negative, like [[cascades-and-surge|cascade RAM]].

Turns a trap build from tempo denial into a RAM engine.
