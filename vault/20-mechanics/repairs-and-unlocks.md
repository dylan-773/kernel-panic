# Repairs and unlocks

> [!warning] status: draft
> Settled with the user on 2026-08-16. This is the spine of both progression and story, and the table below is the single most important unwritten content in the vault.

**Nothing is bought that could be repaired instead.** Every system the player unlocks was already in the shop, broken, left by [[dad]]. The player fixes it, the window comes alive, and the thing they had to move to reach it is an artifact of him.

That is why the pitch says upgrading the shop is how you learn who your father was. One act does three jobs at once.

## Every repair carries three payloads

| Payload | Example |
|---|---|
| **A mechanic** | the solder bay lets you weld [[patch-pieces|pieces]] |
| **A window** | [[solder-bay]] stops being dead |
| **An artifact** | the lesson he taught you at that bench, in his handwriting |

A repair missing the third payload is an upgrade, not a repair, and it should be reconsidered before it ships. See [[ruling-16-reveals-are-upgrade-keyed]].

## Order does not matter

The player chooses what to fix, so no artifact may assume another has been read. Every note stands alone, and the only hard ceiling is that nothing before a win on [[the-machine]] states what is inside it. See [[reveal-schedule]].

This is a real constraint on writing and a cheap one to honour: each artifact is a thing that existed before the player found it, so it has no reason to reference the player's progress at all.

## Where a repair is read

At the object. The archive window keeps every artifact for re-reading, but the first read happens in the room, standing over the thing that was hiding it. See [[the-shop-floor]].

## Open questions

- [ ] How many repairs, and what do they cost? The list above is roughly seven; the shop should probably support twice that.
- [ ] Do repairs cost credits, materials, a day, or a combination?
- [ ] Are there prerequisite chains, or is everything available from the start at rising prices? Proposed: flat and expensive, because a dependency tree quietly restores the linear order the design just removed.
- [ ] Which repairs are cosmetic only? A shop that visibly improves without every improvement being mechanical is worth the art.
- [ ] Does the back room have repairs of its own?
