# Voice and copy laws

Hard laws. A gate verdict may block copy on any of these and cite this note.

## Register

Retro terminal. **Clipped, diegetic, concrete. Short sentences.**

## The dash law

> [!warning] NEVER em dashes or en dashes in player-facing copy
> Periods, commas, or "..." only. This is CLAUDE.md iron rule 6 and it is absolute.
>
> This vault extends the rule to all its own prose, so any line can be lifted into the game without laundering. See [[vault-conventions]].

## Capitalization

- Journal titles and augment names are **ALL CAPS**.
- Day lines open **"DAY N."**
- System voice is short caps declaratives: "RECOVERED SECTOR 1 OF 9.", "PLAYBACK FOLLOWS."

## Names

- The sister is **Rhea**.
- The companion is **Patch**.
- The father is only ever **"Dad"**.
- No given name or surname for the son, Dad, or Rhea's family line, ever, in any copy including official documents. [[ruling-08-names]].

## Per character

| Character | Rule |
|---|---|
| [[dad]] | Workbench wisdom, never exposition dumps. Patient, wry, no self-pity. |
| [[patch]] | Warm precision, light competitiveness. **Only after the finale.** |
| [[rhea]] | Says "the virus" until her run-8 ender renounces it. After that, ironic reference only ("Some virus."). |
| [[the-player]] | Wry, grieving, methodical. Calls Patch "it", always. |

## Customers

**Customers are fond of their machines.** The intrusion is a betrayal, not an inconvenience.

- Win lines: relief, with personality.
- Loss lines: **one cold sentence** in the shop's ledger voice.

See [[customers]].

## Artifact voice

Per [[ruling-12-dad-log-reframe]]: an artifact prints only what its own diegetic source could plausibly print. Inferences, comparisons and reactions belong in the player's bench annotation, typographically subordinate, never in the artifact body.
