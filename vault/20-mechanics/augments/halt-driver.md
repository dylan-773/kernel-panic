# HALT DRIVER

**Config** · `cfgArmHalt` · unlocks [[arm-halt]] on [[attack]]

> ATTACK config: plant halt traps. A sprung trap costs the intrusion its whole next turn.

Unlocks the mode; does not switch to it. Select it in [[loadout-cfg]].

Enables [[tripwire]] in the draft pool.
