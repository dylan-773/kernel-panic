# Ruling 16 - REVEALS ARE UPGRADE KEYED

*Decided 2026-08-16, user call, during the day-as-run redesign.*

Story arrives when the player **repairs something**. Each repair is hand-authored with a specific artifact of [[dad]]'s behind it: the thing he wrote, kept, or was billed for, found where he left it. See [[repairs-and-unlocks]].

**Order is the player's and the writing must not assume one.** Every artifact stands alone. No fragment may reference another fragment as already read, count how many the player has, or describe itself as arriving late or early.

This is cheap to honour because it is true in the fiction: each artifact existed before the player found it and has no idea anyone is coming.

**The single ceiling, which replaces the old knowledge ladder:** nothing before a win against [[the-machine]] may state what is inside it. Everything else, the illness, the hours, the bills, the fact that he built something, may land in any order.

**Forbids:** `unlockAtRun` or any key derived from a count of attempts, days or failures; any artifact whose meaning depends on reading order; any pre-win line naming [[patch]] or confirming the occupant speaks.

**Permits:** the player assembling the truth in their own sequence, and reaching it faster by fixing more of the shop.

**Supersedes** the clause in [[ruling-12-dad-log-reframe]] protecting the `unlockAtRun` key. The rest of ruling 12 stands, and the redesign vindicates it: "artifacts that already existed" is exactly what a repair turns up.
