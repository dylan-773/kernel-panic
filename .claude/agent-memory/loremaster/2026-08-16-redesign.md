On 2026-08-16 the game was redesigned around three user directives, recorded as rulings 15, 16,
17 in `vault/60-story/canon-rulings.md`:

- **The sister (Rhea) is cut entirely.** No sibling, ever, in any content ([[cite-any-canon-status-note]]
  covers how this is enforced at the gate). Rulings 2 and 7 (old cover-story and timeline rulings)
  were retired outright because both rested on her; their live halves moved to rulings 17 and 15.
- **The seal is software, not a padlock.** The back room door is always open and unremarkable;
  what bars the player is a software lock on the drive, opened only by "A FAIR WIN, NO ASSISTS".
  Ruling 15 also carries "no dust": the shop is buried in Dad's clutter (messy genius), and the gap
  between his death and day one is short (days to weeks), not years.
- **Reveals are repair-keyed, not run-keyed.** `unlockAtRun` is gone from `journal.ts`; entries now
  carry `unlock: { kind: "start" | "attempt" | "repair", id: RepairId | "win" }`. The old run-by-run
  knowledge ladder in `reveal-schedule.md` is gone, replaced by a single ceiling ("nothing before a
  win states what is inside the machine") plus eight order-independent prohibitions. As shipped and
  gated 2026-08-19, `journal.ts` holds exactly **ten** entries (will, backroom, failed1, bills,
  solder, receipts, diagnosis, notickets, grading, patch) matching `dad-log-archive.md`'s table
  1:1; there is no `power` entry. `repairs.ts` has **ten** `RepairId`s across eight physical
  stations (`diagBench` is staged 1-3), and seven of the ten carry a recovered sector (1-7, all
  assigned) instead of or alongside a journal entry - see [[ruling-21-artifact-payload-channels]]
  for the rule that legitimizes sector-only or firstRead-only artifact payloads.

**Why this matters:** any future gating or canonization pass on this game should expect the vault
at `vault/60-story/` plus the adjacent `vault/20-mechanics/repairs-and-unlocks.md` and
`vault/20-mechanics/sunday.md` to be current for this design, and should NOT reintroduce sister,
padlock, run-keyed unlocks, or "years of dust" framing from older canon.

**How to apply:** when reading old proposals, code comments, or memory that predates 2026-08-16,
treat sibling/padlock/run-count references as stale, not canon.
