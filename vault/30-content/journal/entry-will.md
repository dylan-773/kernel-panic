# THE WILL

`WILL.SCN` · SCAN · **unlockAtRun 0** · kind `note`

*Provenance: scanned paper, found taped inside the register, folded in four.*

## What it carries

The inheritance, and the division of the shop: counter to [[rhea]], bench to [[the-player]].

> You take the bench. You are bad with people and she is bad with computers. Between the two of you there is exactly one whole shopkeeper.

And the line the whole game hangs on:

> The back room stays locked until it does not.

## Why it is available at run 0

It is the premise, not a reveal. The player starts knowing they were given a bench and refused a room.

Note the artifact carries **no name**, for anyone. [[ruling-08-names]] applies to legal paperwork like everything else, which is a real constraint on a will.
