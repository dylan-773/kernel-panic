# Emeric Snow

**Ivora chess cabinet** · tiers 4, 5 · dominant [[purge]] · `emeric-snow`

> It stopped playing chess and started playing him.

## Appearance

*Canon, ruled 2026-08-19, [[ruling-19-six-customer-appearances]].* Elderly chess club regular, cardigan buttoned off by one, glasses on a chain, unsettled by something he cannot name.

## The dive

A top-tier purge opponent: your traps do not stick. Against a self-cleaning routine, [[arm-halt]] and [[arm-siphon]] are wasted casts and [[redirect]] is the only attack worth making.

At tier 5 it also has every other mode. See [[difficulty-dials]].

## The closest echo in the roster

A machine built to play a game that stops playing the game and starts playing **the person**. That is [[patch]], precisely, described by a customer who has no idea.

Of the twelve, this is the one whose complaint the player will reread after the finale.
