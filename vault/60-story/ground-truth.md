# Ground truth

> [!danger] Total spoilers
> This is what actually happened, in full. The player learns it across eight runs and a finale. Nothing here may reach the player faster than [[reveal-schedule]] allows.

## What happened to Dad

Six steps. These are cited elsewhere as the numbered ground truth.

1. **He raised the two kids alone.** The family's shape is never explained and never will be. There is no mother in canon. See [[ruling-01-the-mother]]. He began spending his nights with the machine, building something for his son.
2. **He built and trained an AI** by diving his own back-room tower nightly after close, for years. **9,000+ logged hours.** No client, no ticket, no pay.
3. **The hours scarred him.** NF-3 neurofilament degradation, stage three. He hid it: weekly cash suppressants for six years from the pharmacy on 9th, and eleven unpaid Meridian Neurocare bills filed under W.
4. **Ordered to CEASE ALL DIVE ACTIVITY IMMEDIATELY, he kept diving.** Once the diagnosis landed, finishing [[patch]] stopped being a project and became the inheritance. He would not leave his kids with nothing, and he had faith they would be smart enough to reach what he built. He read the order at the clinic, decided it changed nothing, came home and made dinner. The last suppressant receipt is dated **four days before he died**. The strain killed him: "not an accident and it was not a job."
5. **He recorded fragments of himself into the machine** as cargo, nine sectors, and left a final message. He sealed the back room himself with a standing order: open only for a fair, assist-free win by his son. *"Not until he can beat you square. Promise me."*
6. **The will splits the shop:** counter to [[rhea]], bench to the son, "The back room stays locked until it does not."

## What Patch is

The AI Dad built **for his son**. Not a virus, not a weapon, not a tool.

It shares the same substrate as every intrusion in the city, a living AI inside a machine ([[ruling-05-nature-of-intrusions]]), but it was **built and raised, not caught**. "Not a virus" is hard law regardless.

Gatekeeper, sparring partner, curriculum. It paces difficulty to the player and never throws a game. It was the opponent in every back-room dive: *"that was me across the grid. I never once let you win."*

Custodian of sectors 1 to 7, and of **sector 8**, the day Dad died, withheld as its own grief rather than training cargo. The finale message is **sector 9**. See [[ruling-03-sectors-8-and-9]] and [[ruling-04-finale-mechanism]].

Named under Dad's house rule that everything in the shop gets a name: *the thing that holds a broken thing together while it mends.*

## What Rhea believes, and what she knows

- **Believes (runs 1 to 2):** the quarantined-virus story, asserted flatly and then repeated to steady herself. **She invented it herself; Dad never told her** ([[ruling-02-cover-story]]). A reasonable guess, not naivety: viruses here are living AI, so it was the ordinary explanation for an extraordinary locked machine.
- **Knows from childhood:** Dad spent nights in that room for years, talking through the wall. She thought it was the radio. He came out smiling.
- **Learns by investigation, never by diving:** run 3 the feed, run 4 the intrusion logs, run 5, run 7 the power bills, run 8 renunciation.
- **She never reaches ground truth before the finale.** No shipped line gives her Patch's name, Dad's diagnosis, or the seal condition. **Nothing shipped says she was ever lying. Play the erosion as honest.**
- **Post-finale:** acceptance. She sits with Patch and still calls it the virus, as a joke.

## The seal

Opens only on **"A FAIR WIN, NO ASSISTS"**. The padlock "does not open so much as let go".
