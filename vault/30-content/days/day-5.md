## minPd arrives

The first day with a floor on the player's opening route cost. By day 5 a kitted player has enough boosts, banked [[cascades-and-surge|cascade]] RAM and [[patch-pieces|pieces]] that an opening burst could otherwise close a short board outright. `minPd` stops the generator handing out a board that can be solved on turn one.

**The hardest day of the first half**, at ~56%. Lower than day 6.

## Story beat

Run 5's ender is sector 4, "keep the door shut", and [[entry-diagnosis|THE DIAGNOSIS]] unlocks at run 5. The halfway day is also where the player can first infer that Dad built the test and that diving killed him. See [[reveal-schedule]].
