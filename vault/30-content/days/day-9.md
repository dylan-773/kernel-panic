## The hardest paying day

Every curve is at its working-day extreme: the largest head start, the tightest par margin, the most slag, the fewest drops, and the first **tier 5** ticket.

A tier 5 opponent runs **all three attack modes and all three defend modes**, casting three targets wide (`oppKitFor` at tier 5, `oppTier` 3). It has the complete vocabulary. See [[difficulty-dials]].

`parFlat` at 1 means par is essentially the route cost. Any inefficiency at all bills [[neural-strain]], on the day the player most needs to arrive at the finale with strain in hand.

## The real decision of day 9

Whether to take the tier 5 ticket at all. It pays 165, the most in the game, and it is the likeliest single source of a run-ending strain bill the night before the finale.
