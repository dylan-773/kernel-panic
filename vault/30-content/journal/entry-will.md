# THE WILL

`WILL.SCN` · SCAN · **available from the start** · kind `note`

*Provenance: scanned paper, found taped inside the register, folded in four.*

## What it carries

The inheritance: the whole shop to [[the-player]], counter and bench both, with no one to share either with.

> The whole thing is yours. I am sorry about the counter.

That line replaces the will's old division of labour between two children, and it has to do the same work in one sentence: the son is a bench person, he is getting the counter anyway, and his father knew it and left it to him regardless.

And the line the whole game hangs on:

> The back room stays locked until it does not.

## Why it is available from the start

It is the premise, not a reveal. The player starts knowing they were given everything and refused one room.

Note the artifact carries **no name**, for anyone. [[ruling-08-names]] applies to legal paperwork like everything else, which is a real constraint on a will.
