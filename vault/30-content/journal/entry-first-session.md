# IT SHUT THE DOOR

`SESSION_001.LOG` · LOG · **the first attempt at the tower** · kind `memo`

*Provenance: tower telemetry, first dive, tonight.*

## What it carries

The tower's own record of the opening attempt, the one the game starts with and never repeats. The machine did not fight. **It graded him and shut the door.**

The first evidence that the thing in the back room behaves unlike an intrusion. An intrusion attacks. This one assessed.

## Why it is telemetry and not a diary page

Because the player did not write it: the tower did. It is the first thing in the archive and it establishes the reader's whole conceit before anything else can. See [[ruling-12-dad-log-reframe]].
