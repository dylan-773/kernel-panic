# Dad

**Only ever "Dad" in copy.** No given name, no surname, ever. Hard law: [[ruling-08-names]].

Dead before the game begins. He is present only as artifacts, recordings, and the machine he left.

## Who he was

Ran the shop and raised the two kids alone. The family's shape is never explained and never will be ([[ruling-01-the-mother]]). A messy genius: the shop is buried in his clutter.

**Secretly a diver of enormous hours.** 9,000+ logged, nightly after close, for years, on his own tower. No client, no ticket, no pay. He was building [[patch]] for his son.

## What it cost him

The diving caused the illness. NF-3 neurofilament degradation, stage three. Weekly cash suppressants for six years from the pharmacy on 9th. Eleven unpaid Meridian Neurocare bills, filed under W "for whatever".

Read **CEASE ALL DIVE ACTIVITY IMMEDIATELY** at the clinic, decided it changed nothing, came home and made dinner.

Last suppressant receipt: **four days before he died.**

> [!info] The central rhyme
> [[neural-strain]] is the player's health bar and the father's cause of death. The same mechanic on two timescales. The game never says this out loud.

## Why he kept going

Once the diagnosis landed, finishing Patch stopped being a project and became **the inheritance**. He would not leave his kids with nothing, and he had faith they would be smart enough to reach what he built.

## The seal

He sealed the back room himself, with one condition: **not until the boy can beat Patch square. No shortcuts.**

> Not until he can beat you square. Promise me.

## Voice

Patient, wry, workbench koans, **no self-pity**.

> The joint holds because you were patient with it. Most things do.

> it only sticks where you have cleaned

Fragments are workbench wisdom, never exposition dumps. See [[voice-and-copy-laws]].

## The house rule

Everything that lives in this shop gets a name. It is why the tower's volume is DAD.VOL, and why [[patch]] is called Patch.
