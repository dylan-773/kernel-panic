# The player - the son

**Unnamed on screen, permanently.** No given name, no surname, ever, for him or any family member. Hard law: [[ruling-08-names]].

## The will

> You take the bench. You are bad with people and she is bad with computers. Between the two of you there is exactly one whole shopkeeper.

## Who he is

A natural diver. Learned patience at Dad's bench as a child, in the soldering lesson:

> it only sticks where you have cleaned

Reads [[dad-log-archive|DAD.LOG]], the recovered archive of Dad's own files, mounted and read file by file. **His own voice survives only as terse bench annotations attached to a file**, never as the artifact's primary text ([[ruling-12-dad-log-reframe]]).

## Voice

Wry, grieving, methodical.

Refers to [[patch]] as **"it"** in his own bench notes, even after the finale. [[rhea]] says "he". That difference is characterization, not inconsistency: he is the one who spent nine days being graded by it.

## Constraints

- Never named.
- Never pictured. There is no player portrait asset.
- Never speaks in the third person.
- His inferences and reactions belong in bench annotations, never in an artifact's printed body.
