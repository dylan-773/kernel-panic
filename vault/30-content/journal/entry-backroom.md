# THE BACK ROOM

`TICKET_QUERY.LOG` · LOG · **unlockAtRun 0** · kind `memo`

*Provenance: shop system query, bench terminal, day one.*

## What it carries

The player queried the shop's ticketing system for the tower and found nothing:

> NO ENTRY IN THIS SYSTEM, EVER

A machine in a repair shop with no ticket, no owner and no service record. It was never a job.

## The clarification that ruling 13 exists for

> [!warning] "NO ENTRY IN THIS SYSTEM, EVER" refers to the shop's **separate** ticketing system finding no ticket
> It is **not** a claim that the tower's own drive has no files. DAD.VOL is the tower's local storage and is full. See [[ruling-13-dad-vol-provenance]].

Without that ruling the two facts read as contradictory, since the player is reading this entry off the very volume it appears to deny.
