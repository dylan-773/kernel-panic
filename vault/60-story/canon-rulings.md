## Two rulings were deleted by the redesign

**2 (cover story)** and **7 (timeline)** both rested on the sister and the padlock, neither of which exists. Ruling 2's live half, that the virus story is an honest guess rather than a lie anyone told, is now [[ruling-17-the-players-own-assumption]]. Ruling 7's live half, that the shop has no dust because the gap between the death and the first day is short, is carried in [[ruling-15-the-seal-is-software]]. Numbers 2 and 7 are retired and will not be reused.

## The shape of a ruling

Each carries what it **permits** and what it **forbids**. The forbids clause is the operative half: it is what a gate verdict cites when it blocks an artifact.

Two origins:
- **User calls and directives** (1, 3 to 6, 8, 12, 14 to 17) - decided by the user, sometimes recorded verbatim.
- **Gate rulings** (9, 10, 11, 13, 18 to 21) - the [[loremaster]] closing a gap a cycle exposed. Permitted by its charter: if a proposal exposes a genuine gap in canon, decide it, add the ruling, then gate against it.
