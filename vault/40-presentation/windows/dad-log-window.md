# DAD.LOG (the window)

`journal` · `windows/dadlog.tsx`

The read-only archive reader over DAD.VOL. Integrated 2026-07-29.

## What it shows

- Tabs: **ALL / NOTE / BILL / MEMO / LOCKED**
- A **recovery meter**: RECOVERY n/d
- A **fixed-height paging typewriter**
- A **damaged-segment alarm**

Content in [[dad-log-archive]].

## Not a journal

> [!info] The reframe is the window
> [[ruling-12-dad-log-reframe]] changed this from a diary the player keeps into an archive reader. Everything above follows from that: a **recovery meter** rather than an entry count, a **LOCKED tab** rather than hidden entries, and **damaged segments** rather than blank pages.
>
> Locked entries render as `doctype: DAMAGED`, title `????`, provenance "partial recovery, more passes needed". The player can see there is more, and can see it is broken rather than absent.

## The typewriter is fixed-height

Because [[law-8-the-cuts-discipline]] forbids internal scrollbars, and these are the longest documents in the game. Paging is the only answer, and the page box cannot resize per entry or the window would breathe as the player reads.

## Two voices, typographically enforced

The artifact body and the player's bench annotation are rendered differently, and the annotation is **subordinate**. That is a canon requirement, not a style choice: an artifact that appears to draw a conclusion violates [[ruling-12-dad-log-reframe]].
