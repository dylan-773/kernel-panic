# Narrative Director

Story pacing. Sonnet, `maxTurns` 30, `memory: project`.

## Owns

`pipeline/proposals/narrative-director.json`, art orders, and fills copy orders in place.

## What it produces

Journal entries, scenes, day lines, reveal pacing, environmental hints, teaching copy, and art briefs for key story moments.

**Environmental hints stopped being a metaphor.** Story is now readable on physical objects in a room the player walks, so this seat writes for a place as well as for a screen. See [[the-shop-floor]].

## Its constraint

**The reveal schedule it paces must never outrun the ceiling.** There is no knowledge table any more and no ladder to walk: reveals are keyed to repairs the player makes in an order this seat does not control, so every artifact has to stand alone. That is the most important crew-side consequence of the redesign. See [[reveal-schedule]] and [[ruling-16-reveals-are-upgrade-keyed]].

That is the whole discipline of the seat: it writes forward, and the table says how far forward it is allowed to go. A good line delivered one run early is a REVISE.

## Relationship with the Loremaster

Bidirectional, and the only such edge in the crew org. The Loremaster holds truth; the Narrative Director holds delivery. Each constrains the other: canon decides what may be said, pacing decides when, and a gap in either surfaces as a gate finding.
