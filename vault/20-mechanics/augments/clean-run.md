# CLEAN RUN

**Boost** · `cleanRun`

> Win a dive with zero strain billed and bank one random patch piece. A trap-free win that only misses at the round cap pays 15 credits instead.

Two payouts, both rewarding precision:

- **Zero strain billed** on a win banks a random [[patch-pieces|piece]]. Zero means every term in the strain formula came out at nothing: at or under par, no traps sprung, no redirects taken, no pressure rounds. See [[route-cost-and-par]].
- **A trap-free [[the-round-cap|cap]] win** pays 15 credits as a consolation.

The only augment that pays out on how well you played rather than what you did.
