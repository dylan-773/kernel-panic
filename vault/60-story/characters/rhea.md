# Rhea - the sister

Runs the counter, keeps the books, keeps her brother alive: coffee, soup, dragging him off the bench. **Never dives.** Bad with computers by the will's own account.

Her given name stays in canon. Her family's surname does not. See [[ruling-08-names]].

## What she does instead of diving

Investigates by shopkeeper means. Watches the feed. Reads intrusion logs start to finish, twice. Does the math on the old power bills.

That is the design: she reaches most of the truth without ever touching the grid, which is what makes her a second, parallel investigation rather than a bystander.

## Voice

Dry, protective, deflects feeling into logistics.

> I oiled the hinges on the back door. Do not read into it.

## The virus theory

**She invented the quarantine cover story herself. Dad never told her.** [[ruling-02-cover-story]].

It was a reasonable guess, not naivety: viruses in this world are living AI, so a quarantined virus was the ordinary explanation for an extraordinary locked-off machine ([[ruling-05-nature-of-intrusions]]).

> [!warning] Play the erosion as honest
> Nothing shipped says she was ever lying. She is wrong, and she is wrong for good reasons, and she updates when the evidence arrives.

## Her arc, and its fixed schedule

| Run | Beat |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | Believes. Asserts it flatly, then repeats it to steady herself: "That is all it has to be." |
| 3 | Watches the feed. It waited. |
| 4 | Reads the intrusion logs. It paces. |
| 5 | It learns, and it is kind first. |
| 7 | The power bills. "You do not do that for a virus." |
| 8 | Renounces: "I do not think it is a virus... I think it has been waiting." |
| 9+ | "One of these nights it is going to let you through. I have started believing that." |
| finale | Acceptance. She sits with [[patch]]. |

**Do not accelerate it.** See [[reveal-schedule]].

## Copy law

She says "the virus" until the run-8 ender renounces it. After that she may only reference it ironically:

> Some virus.

Post-finale, on Patch: "He laughs at the edges the way Dad did." She says **"he"**.
