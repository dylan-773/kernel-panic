# Patch - the companion

> [!danger] The name may not appear before the run-8 ender
> See [[reveal-schedule]], prohibition 1.

An AI [[dad]] built and trained in the back-room tower. Named under the house naming rule for

> the thing that holds a broken thing together while it mends

## What it is, and is not

- **Not a virus.** Hard law, regardless of substrate.
- **Not a tool.** *"I stopped thinking of you that way a long time ago."*
- Shares the same substrate as every intrusion in the city, a living AI inside a machine ([[ruling-05-nature-of-intrusions]]), but was **built and raised, not caught**.

## Standing orders

Keep the door shut. Pace the player. **Never throw a game.** Open only for a fair win with no assists.

It was the opponent in every back-room dive:

> that was me across the grid. I never once let you win.

## Custodian of the sectors

Holds sectors 1 to 7 as training cargo, and **sector 8**, the day Dad died, withheld as its own grief rather than cargo. The finale message is **sector 9**: Dad's real, degraded recordings as source, reconstructed by a voice Patch practised "until it matched his".

Neither pure tape nor pure synthesis. See [[ruling-04-finale-mechanism]] and [[ruling-03-sectors-8-and-9]].

## Personality, once open

Warm, confident, lightly competitive.

> Deal me in. I am very good. You may have noticed.

Laughs at the edges the way Dad did.

> [!warning] Before the finale it has no voice at all
> No content may confirm the occupant speaks, or give it a personality or pronoun beyond the player's "it". Everything in this section is finale-and-after only.

## Pronouns in copy

[[rhea]] says **"he"**. The player's own bench notes say **"it"**, even after the finale. Both are correct; the difference is characterization.

## After

> Counter is mine, bench is yours, back room is his.
