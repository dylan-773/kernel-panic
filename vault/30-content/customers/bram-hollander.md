# Bram Hollander

**Copperline register hub** · tiers 2, 3 · dominant [[lock]] · `bram-hollander`

> His own register walls the till off from him.

## Appearance

*Canon, ruled 2026-07-29.* Proud, weathered shopkeeper, apron at his own counter, eleven years in that till.

![[repairlog-figure-bram.png]]

## The dive

A clamp job. Junctions you need freeze solid for two rounds, which is the most frustrating denial in the game if you have no answer, and trivial if you do.

The answer is a SURGE: light six or more nodes at once and every enemy lock on the board shatters. Bram's ticket is where a player learns that [[cascades-and-surge|cascades]] are not only a RAM engine. See [[lock]].

## The echo

A shopkeeper locked out of his own register, by his own register. The player is a shopkeeper locked out of his own back room, by his own father. The game does not comment.
