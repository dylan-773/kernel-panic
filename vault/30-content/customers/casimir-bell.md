# Casimir Bell

**Ledgerstone pawn vault** · tiers 4, 5 · dominant [[lock]] · `casimir-bell`

> The vault grew a lock nobody bought.

## Appearance

Not yet ruled. **Invent nothing without a ruling.**

## The dive

Top-tier clamps. At `DEFEND_WIDTH` 3 it freezes three junctions per cast for two rounds each, which can stall a route entirely if the player has no [[cascades-and-surge|cascade]] ready.

## The line

"A lock nobody bought" is the whole game in five words. There is one of those in the back room too, and the player has been trying to open it since run 1.

Of the eleven customer hooks that echo the main story, this is the least disguised, which is presumably why it sits at the top of the tier band where the player is already thinking about day 10.
