# Game goals

> [!warning] status: draft
> The **what** and **how** are settled and read out of the shipped game. The **who** has never been written down anywhere in the repo. What follows is my reading of who the game is built for, based on what it is. Correct it.

## What

A finished, playable browser roguelike: tutorial, nine-day arc, finale, story, sound and saves, all live at kernel-panic.higgsfield.app. Build 0.7.0.

It is also, in its second life, the artifact of a multi-agent development experiment. See [[the-dev-crew]] and [[token-budget]].

## Who

*Draft.* The game assumes a player who:

- will read a diagnostic before committing, and enjoys that the tell is honest;
- reads **losing as content** rather than as punishment;
- likes systems that are small enough to hold entirely in their head. Three programs, six modes, eighteen augments;
- is comfortable with a deliberately retro, text-dense interface and does not need a 3D scene.

That points at the same audience as Into the Breach and Slay the Spire: players who want a short, legible, deterministic tactical puzzle with a reason to replay. The story audience is narrower and quieter, and is the reason the game is not just a puzzle.
