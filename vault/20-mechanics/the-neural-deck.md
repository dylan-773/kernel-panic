# The neural deck

> [!warning] status: draft
> Settled with the user on 2026-08-16. Replaces the deleted boost-bays note as the owner of the player's build.

The rig the player dives with. Everything the player **is** in a duel comes from here: [[ram]] per turn, [[program-tiers|program tiers]], which modes they can cast, and how many [[augments]] they can carry.

It was dad's. The first upgrades are repairs.

## Its currency is salvage

Deck upgrades are not bought with [[credits]]. They are built from parts pulled out of customer machines and the darknet, which puts the combat layer and the build layer on the same loop: diving is how you get better at diving, and credits stay for the shop.

Salvage earned in a day is **held**, not banked, until the day closes. A deck upgrade is a reason to stop while ahead. See [[day-close-and-banking]].

## Unlocks are permanent, slots are not

The redesign's central swap:

| | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Acquiring an augment | the choice, made once per ticket, lost at run end | permanent the moment the day banks |
| Carrying an augment | automatic, up to the bay count | **the choice**, made in [[loadout-cfg]] before a dive |

An augment the player has drafted is theirs forever. The deck has a limited number of slots, so a full catalog does not mean a full loadout: it means a large menu and a small plate.

This solves the problem the old design flagged and never fixed. The augment pool used to run dry partway through the arc and progression simply stopped. Under permanent unlocks the pool runs dry sooner and stays dry, which would be worse, except that acquisition is no longer where the interest is. See [[augments]].

## Why this makes diagnosis matter

Slots are only a decision if the player knows what they are configuring against. That is what ties the deck to the shop: diagnosis depth grows with the bench, so a better-equipped shop reads more of the intrusion before the dive, and a better read makes the loadout screen a puzzle instead of a preference. See [[scan]] and [[the-counter]].

A player with no diagnosis and eight slots is guessing. A player with a full readout and four slots is playing.

## Open questions

- [ ] How many slots, and what is the curve? The interesting range is one where the player is always leaving something good behind.
- [ ] Do RAM and tiers consume slots, or are they flat capacity upgrades outside the slot economy? Proposed: outside, so slots stay purely about augments and modes.
- [ ] Are modes slotted too? If they are, the counter triangle becomes a loadout decision, which is attractive and also the fastest way to make a dive unwinnable by miscofiguration.
- [ ] Can the loadout change between customers in a day, or is it committed at the counter when the job is taken? Committing at intake makes the read matter more and the day riskier.
- [ ] What are salvage's denominations, and do different device types yield different parts?
