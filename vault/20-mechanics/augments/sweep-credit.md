# SWEEP CREDIT

**Boost** · `sweepCredit`

> PURGE refunds 1 RAM per trap it defuses, up to 3 per cast.

At DEFEND tier 3 against a trap-heavy board, a purge that clears 3 traps refunds 3 RAM against a 1 RAM cast, so it is RAM positive.

Answers the steepest exchange in the game: [[scan]] then [[purge]] is two programs and two turns to undo one enemy cast. This makes the second half free.
