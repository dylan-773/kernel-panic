## The first real change

**horizon rises to 1.** The machine starts scoring twists by how much they raise the player's [[route-cost-and-par|route cost]]. This is the moment interference enters the game, and it is the whole of the old AI arriving at once.

`abilityFreq` doubles from 0.20 to 0.40, so casts roughly double in frequency.

The day line does the teaching the interface does not: **strain carries over**. It is the first statement that the run is a resource arc rather than a series of independent jobs.

The highest target win rate of the arc, at ~93%. Day 2 is where the player should feel competent.
