# NO TICKETS

`LEDGER_XREF.QRY` · QUERY · **repair: the ledger terminal** · kind `memo`

*Provenance: ledger cross reference, run twice to be sure.*

## What it carries

Cross-referenced against the shop's ledger: **no client ever existed**. The hours, the strain and the death were for something he was building in secret, on an installment plan.

## "Run twice to be sure"

The provenance line is the emotional content. The player did not believe the first result.

It is also a callback: [[entry-backroom]] ran the same query against the ticketing system and came back empty too. On its own that is a curiosity. Against the ledger it is confirmation of something much worse.

The two have to work in either order, since the player may reach this one first. Neither refers to the other, and both simply report what they found.

## The power bills do the same job elsewhere

The strongest corroboration in the game is not a dive log, it is arithmetic on paper: years of power draw from a room with no client attached to it. **You do not do that for a virus.** That evidence used to be spoken by the sister; it is now an artifact the player finds and does the sum on themselves, behind an electrical repair.
