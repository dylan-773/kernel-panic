# Narrative Director

Story pacing. Sonnet, `maxTurns` 30, `memory: project`.

## Owns

`pipeline/proposals/narrative-director.json`, art orders, and fills copy orders in place.

## What it produces

Journal entries, run scenes, day lines, reveal pacing, environmental hints, teaching copy, and art briefs for key story moments.

## Its constraint

**The reveal schedule it paces must never outrun the knowledge table.** See [[reveal-schedule]].

That is the whole discipline of the seat: it writes forward, and the table says how far forward it is allowed to go. A good line delivered one run early is a REVISE.

## Relationship with the Loremaster

Bidirectional, and the only such edge in the crew org. The Loremaster holds truth; the Narrative Director holds delivery. Each constrains the other: canon decides what may be said, pacing decides when, and a gap in either surfaces as a gate finding.
