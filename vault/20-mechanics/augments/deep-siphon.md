# DEEP SIPHON

**Boost** · `siphonPlus` · requires [[siphon-driver]]

> Your siphon traps steal 1 extra RAM.

Stacks on `SIPHON_STEAL`, so at ATTACK tier 3 a siphon moves 5 RAM.

Gated by `{ kind: "augment", id: "cfgArmSiphon" }`, so the draft never offers it to a player who cannot plant siphons.
