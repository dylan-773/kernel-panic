# RECEIPTS

`RECEIPTS.SCN` · SCAN · **repair: storage and shelving** · kind `bill`

*Provenance: scanned paper, shoebox, pharmacy on 9th, six years of stubs.*

## What it carries

Strain suppressants. Weekly. Cash. **Six years.** Climbing dosage.

The last stub is dated **four days before he died**.

## Why a shoebox of stubs is the strongest artifact in the archive

It proves duration. A diagnosis is a moment; six years of weekly cash receipts is a **practice**, hidden, sustained, at cost, while running a shop and raising two kids.

The artifact itself says none of that. It is a stack of pharmacy stubs with dates and amounts. The inference belongs in the bench annotation. See [[ruling-12-dad-log-reframe]].

## Knowledge ceiling

A shoebox of stubs says the illness was real, hidden and long, and says nothing else. It cannot state the hours or the cease order because a pharmacy receipt does not know them, which is exactly why it is safe to find at any point. See [[reveal-schedule]].
