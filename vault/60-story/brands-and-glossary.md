## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **diving** | linking into a device and fighting what is inside it on a grid. Real, mundane, paid work. |
| **intrusion** | a living AI infecting a machine. Every shop ticket is an intrusion job. See [[intrusions]]. |
| **Neural Strain** | the body's cost of diving. Zero severs the connection: a blackout, not a death. See [[neural-strain]]. |

| **sectors** | numbered recovered cargo, 1 to 9. Seven surface before the finale. |
| **the tower** | the back-room machine. See [[the-machine]]. |

> [!warning] Night Patch price drift
> `lore/bible.md` calls Night Patch "the 60-credit strain restore". Shipped, it costs `45 + 5 * day`, so 50 on day 1 rising to 90 on day 9. The flat 60 is stale. See [[the-night-shop]].

## The lowercase coincidence

"patch" appears as an ordinary noun (Night Patch, patch pieces, patching a board) long before [[patch]] is named. **The coincidence is allowed to sit there unremarked before the finale.** It is not a clue and must never be pointed at.
