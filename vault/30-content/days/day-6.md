## The augment pool runs dry

> [!warning] The arc's known structural problem
> One draft per cleared ticket, three a day: by day 6 the player has had up to 18 picks, which is the entire catalog. Days 7 to 9 offer 25 credits of [[credits|salvage]] instead of progression.
>
> The hardest third of the run has **no build progression left**. The v2 GDD flagged this itself and it is still true. See [[augments]].

## Difficulty

`abilityFreq` peaks at 0.78, higher than any later day including day 9. The machine casts more often here than it ever will again; what rises later is intelligence, not frequency.

First day where all three tickets are tier 3.
