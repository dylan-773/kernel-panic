## horizon 3

The machine reaches full intelligence. It now:

- weights cuts by **what they cost the player to repair**, so it deliberately twists elbows and tees over straights;
- runs `assessRace`, comparing the two clocks in turns;
- **will abandon its own race to deny the player's** when the player's clock is shorter.

That last behaviour is the qualitative change. Below horizon 3 the machine is always trying to win. At horizon 3 it is sometimes trying to make you lose.

## The day line is the mechanic

"Strain is a budget. Spend it like rent is due." By day 8 a kitted player is at roughly a coin flip per ticket, with a tight par margin and no [[augments|augment]] progression. See [[neural-strain]].

## Story beat

[[entry-grading|IT IS GRADING ME]] unlocks at run 8, and run 8's ender is sector 7: the naming. This is the run where [[rhea]] renounces the virus theory and the player concludes "Dad did not seal something in. He left something waiting."
