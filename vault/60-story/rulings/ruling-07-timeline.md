# Ruling 7 - TIMELINE

*Decided 2026-07-25, user call.*

**No dust.** The shop is buried in [[dad]]'s clutter because he was a messy genius, and [[rhea]] has been clearing it since he died.

The death-to-run-1 gap is **short: days to weeks**.

The padlock survives as the one deliberately cared-for thing amid the mess.

**Forbids:** any "decade of dust" or long post-death gap framing.

**Integration:** two shipped run-1 opener lines were rewritten. They now read "He would have hated how clean I kept it." and "The padlock is the only thing in this shop Dad ever put away properly."
