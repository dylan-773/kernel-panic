# SIPHON DRIVER

**Config** · `cfgArmSiphon` · unlocks [[arm-siphon]] on [[attack]]

> ATTACK config: plant siphon traps. A sprung trap drains RAM from its next turn into yours, more at higher ATTACK tiers, and more again when you are the one springing it.

Steal scales with ATTACK tier: 2, 3, 4 RAM. See [[program-tiers]].

Enables [[deep-siphon]] in the draft pool.
