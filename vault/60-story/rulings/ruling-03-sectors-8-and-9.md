# Ruling 3 - SECTORS 8 AND 9

*Decided 2026-07-25, user call.*

- **Sector 8** is the day [[dad]] died, withheld by [[patch]] as **its own grief**, not training cargo. It never plays as a numbered fragment in runs 1 to 12.
- **Sector 9** is the raw material behind the final message, delivered **whole at the finale**, never staged separately.

**Forbids:** either sector appearing in the run 2 to 8 schedule or any run 9+ fallback cycle.

Nine sectors exist; seven surface before the finale.
