## The dive

A low-tier ward job, which is unusual: warding is prevention, so the intrusion is not attacking so much as refusing to be attacked. Your traps will not land and your redirects will bounce inside the bubble.

The counter is routing around rather than through, which makes this an early lesson that not every job is answered with the same kit.
