# The shop

A family computer repair shop, inherited whole by the son on [[dad]]'s death, per the will taped inside the register.

This note is the canon of the place: what is in it and what each thing carries. For how it is presented and walked, see [[the-shop-floor]].

## Layout

- **The front counter** - where the work comes in. [[the-counter]].
- **The bench** - where the work happens, and where KP/OS lives. [[the-bench-transition]].
- **The back room**, holding [[the-machine|the tower]]. No curtain, no padlock, no lock of any kind on the door. See [[ruling-15-the-seal-is-software]].
- **Upstairs**, where he lives. [[the-bedroom]].

Dad was a messy genius and the shop is buried in his clutter. Clearing it is not housekeeping, it is the game: every thing moved or repaired turns up something of his. See [[repairs-and-unlocks]].

## Props with canon weight

| Prop | Carries |
|---|---|
| the register | the will, taped inside |
| the ticket spike | the jobs taken today, and not yet finished |
| the ledger | the books, and [[ledger-log]] |
| the bottom drawer | eleven Meridian FINAL NOTICEs, filed under W "for whatever" |
| the shoebox | six years of pharmacy receipts |
| the solder bay | the lesson, and crafting once repaired |
| the onion router | his line to the darknet, and how he got parts |
| the tower | the only thing in the shop he ever put away properly |

The pharmacy on 9th is four blocks away.

## The bills

The Meridian balance alone is more than the shop clears in a year, and there is nothing the player can do about it. It is not a mechanic and never comes due: it is the reason [[dad]] kept diving while he was dying. See [[ruling-06-meridian]] for the fact that the clinic and the ledger-hardware brand are one company.

## Rhythm

Six working days, then Sunday, and no last day at all. The shop opens when the player opens it and closes when they close it. See [[day-close-and-banking]] and [[sunday]].

## Dad's house rule

**Everything that lives in this shop gets a name.**

It is why the tower's drive is DAD.VOL and why the thing inside it is called [[patch]].

## No dust

The gap between the death and the first day is short: days to weeks. See [[ruling-15-the-seal-is-software]].
