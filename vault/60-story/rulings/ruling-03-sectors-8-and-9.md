# Ruling 3 - SECTORS 8 AND 9

*Decided 2026-07-25, user call.*

- **Sector 8** is the day [[dad]] died, withheld by [[patch]] as **its own grief**, not training cargo. It never plays as a numbered fragment, at any point, by any trigger.
- **Sector 9** is the raw material behind the final message, delivered **whole on a win**, never staged separately.

**Forbids:** either sector being attached to a repair, a scene, or any other reveal trigger. The findable set is sectors 1 to 7 and no more.

*Amended 2026-08-16: the original wording forbade these appearing in the run 2 to 8 ender schedule, which no longer exists. The prohibition is unchanged in substance and is now stated without reference to a key. See [[ruling-16-reveals-are-upgrade-keyed]].*
