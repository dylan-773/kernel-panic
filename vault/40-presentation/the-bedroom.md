# The bedroom

> [!warning] status: draft
> Settled on 2026-08-16 only as far as: it exists, it is upstairs, the shop is downstairs.

Where the player sleeps, above the shop they inherited. It is small and it should stay small.

## Its one certain job

**Sleeping ends the day.** Going up the stairs and going to bed is how the player closes, which makes the most consequential decision in the game a physical act rather than a button. Everything held becomes banked on the way up. See [[day-close-and-banking]].

On a failed day the player goes up with nothing, which is the same walk and reads completely differently. That is worth more than any lose screen.

## Its likely second job

The evening happens between closing and sleeping: spending, repairing, configuring the deck. Whether that phase is upstairs, downstairs, or at the terminal is open. See [[the-night-shop]].

## Living above the shop

He grew up here. Dad lived here. The room is the only space in the game that is not a workplace, and the only one where an artifact would be personal rather than professional.

## Open questions

- [ ] Is the evening phase up here, or does the player only come up to sleep?
- [ ] Is there anything to interact with, or is the bed the whole room? A room with one verb is honest; a room with one verb the player must walk to is a corridor.
- [ ] Does it change as the shop does? The strongest version is that it does not, and the contrast is the point.
- [ ] Is it where saves happen, in the fiction?
