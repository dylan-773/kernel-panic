Rule: when a shipped line is excised for canon reasons (e.g. a whole
character or event removed), do not reuse that line's load-bearing word or
image in the replacement line for an adjacent beat in the same scene, even
if my replacement no longer states the excised fact directly. The reader
will reconstruct the excised content from the leftover vocabulary alone.

**Why:** On 2026-07-25 the shipped run-3 ender father beat had its first line
"She took the car and the quiet with her." excised (see
[[canon-rulings-2026-07]], mother excised entirely). My first two rewrite
passes both replaced it with lines built around "quiet" ("Quiet house
tonight, I could get used to that." / "Nights around here just got
quieter.") because "quiet" was the emotionally resonant image of the
original line and felt like the natural thing to preserve. The Loremaster
gate rejected both: "quiet" was vocabulary residue from the excised line
and invited the reader to reconstruct the departure, even with the literal
"she" gone. My own rationale for candidate B ("gesture at something having
shifted") was cited back at me as proof the problem was real, not just a
word choice.

**How to apply:** When rewriting a line to remove a person, event, or fact
from canon, treat that line's single most evocative word or image as
radioactive, not just its literal claim. Rebuild the replacement from a
completely different sensory register (a different sense, a different
object, a different frame like "what IS" instead of "what changed") rather
than trying to keep the original's emotional shape with the offending noun
swapped out. Concretely for this game: prefer present-tense physical facts
(a light on, an hour, an object on the bench) over any
before/after/change/absence framing when the line being replaced was about
someone leaving or something ending.
