# The shop

A family computer repair shop, inherited jointly by the two kids on [[dad]]'s death, per the will taped inside the register.

## Layout

- **The front counter** - [[rhea]]'s.
- **The bench** - [[the-player]]'s.
- **A curtained, padlocked back room** holding [[the-machine|the tower]].

Dad was a messy genius; the shop is buried in his clutter, and Rhea has been clearing it since he died. **The padlock is the one thing in the shop that was ever deliberately cared for.**

## Props with canon weight

| Prop | Carries |

The pharmacy on 9th is four blocks away.

## Debt

The Meridian balance alone is more than the shop clears in a year. See [[ruling-06-meridian]] for the fact that the clinic and the ledger-hardware brand are one company.

## Rhythm

**Ten days on the book per attempt.** Nine working days of three tickets each, then day 10, when "the back room settles up". See [[the-ten-day-arc]].

## Dad's house rule

**Everything that lives in this shop gets a name.**

It is why the tower's drive is DAD.VOL and why the thing inside it is called [[patch]].

## No dust

The death-to-run-1 gap is short: days to weeks. See [[ruling-07-timeline]].
