# Ruling 10 - OVERTIME BILLING

*Added 2026-07-28, Loremaster gate 2 on the deep-balance cycle.*

The shop **may bill a cap win at full overtime rate** instead of splitting the loss with the client.

Ordinary trade practice, not exploitative labor framing, and well within motive for a shop this precarious: the Meridian balance alone is more than the shop clears in a year.

**Back office only.** It changes no customer-facing line. Win lines, loss lines and every customer's flavor text are untouched, so it does not contradict "Customers are fond of their machines; the intrusion is a betrayal, not an inconvenience."

**Forbids:** any scene or customer line where a client resents, disputes, or calls out the shop for overbilling. That reading needs its own ruling first.

**Explicitly not a canon question:** a build that farms the bonus by stalling to the [[the-round-cap|round cap]] on purpose is a balance-loop incentive question. No line forbids a player-optimal strategy from reading as hard invoicing.

Implemented as [[overtime-clause]].
