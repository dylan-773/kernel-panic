# The counter

> [!warning] status: draft
> New with the split environment on 2026-08-16. Takes over the front half of what [[inbox]] used to do.

Where the work comes from. A customer walks in, describes what is wrong in the words a person uses, and hands over a device. The player takes the job or does not.

## Intake is a scene, not a list

The old design put three tickets on a board and asked which order to take them in. Intake is now one person at a time, in the room, and the question is no longer order but **whether to take this one at all**. That is the day's real decision and it should happen face to face. See [[day-close-and-banking]].

The twelve profiles survive unchanged and get better here: the One Wow line each customer carries was always written to be spoken. See [[customers]].

## What the counter knows and what the terminal knows

| At the counter | At the terminal |
|---|---|
| who they are, what they say is wrong, what they will pay | the threat tier, the dominant mode, the honest tell |
| the decision to accept | the decision of how to fight |

The complaint is a person's account and can be vague, wrong or embarrassed. The readout is a machine's account and is honest. Keeping them separate is what makes the diagnostic bench worth repairing: a better bench turns more of the first column into the second. See [[scan]] and [[repairs-and-unlocks]].

## Accepted work goes to the queue

[[inbox]] keeps its job. It stops being where work appears and becomes the record of what the player has taken and not yet finished, plus the readout and the DIVE button.

## Open questions

- [ ] Do customers wait, leave, or queue? A customer who leaves after being ignored is a cost with no interface.
- [ ] Can a job be declined without a penalty, and does the player see anything about a device before accepting it?
- [ ] How does a customer arrive: on a timer, on the player returning to the counter, or on the previous job resolving?
- [ ] Can accepted work be carried to the next day, or must every job be finished or failed today?
- [ ] Twelve regulars against an unbounded number of customer-days. Repeats, procedural walk-ins, or many more profiles? See [[encounter-generator]].
- [ ] Do customers appear as figures in the room, or as a portrait at the counter?
